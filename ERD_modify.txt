Doctoralia — ERD_modify.txt
Feature: Doctor Side Schedule Management & Availability

Relationships:

Doctor (1) ──< Doctor_Schedule >── (1) Schedule (N) ──> Hospital (1)
   M           junction            N      N               1

Details:

- Doctor ||--o{ Doctor_Schedule : has
  PK doctor_id, FK department_id, etc.
  Cardinality: 1 doctor has 0..N rows in doctor_schedule.

- Schedule ||--o{ Doctor_Schedule : has
  PK schedule_id, FK hospital_id, available_date, start_time, end_time
  Cardinality: 1 schedule is linked to 0..N rows in doctor_schedule.

- Doctor_Schedule }o--|| Doctor : belongs
  }o--|| Schedule : belongs
  Composite PK (doctor_id, schedule_id)
  Attributes: status ENUM('AVAILABLE','WORKING','UNAVAILABLE') — only these three.
  Reason: status is per doctor+schedule, not per schedule alone.

- Hospital ||--o{ Schedule : has
  PK hospital_id
  Cardinality: 1 hospital has 0..N schedules (1:N).
  FK schedule.hospital_id -> hospital.hospital_id
  Reason: schedule must specify hospital where doctor available.

- Appointment }o--|| Doctor : booked with
  }o--|| Patient : booked by
  }o--|| Schedule (nullable, set by staff) : uses
  }o--|| Hospital (nullable, set by staff) : at
